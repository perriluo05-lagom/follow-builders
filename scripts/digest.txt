# AI Builders Digest — Sunday, July 27, 2026

## 🎙️ Podcast: OpenAI's Compute Chief — We Can't Build Fast Enough

**Source:** The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**Guest:** Sachin Katti (Head of Industrial Compute at OpenAI)
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEZBlmvxx4o

### 📌 核心要点

**1. 算力供需严重失衡**
OpenAI 的算力需求远超供应能力。Sachin 透露，公司今年计划投入约 500 亿美元用于算力建设，而整个行业的算力支出预计达 7000 亿美元。任何上线的算力资源都会立即被消耗，不存在闲置情况。

**2. AI 正在设计自己的芯片**
Jalapeno 芯片仅用 9 个月就完成了从设计到流片的全过程，其中 AI 在设计优化中发挥了关键作用。OpenAI 相信，AI 设计 AI 所需系统的"递归"时代已经不远。

**3. 物理世界的限制**
供应链、工厂建设速度跟不上 AI 需求的增长。变压器、燃气轮机等基础设施产能严重不足，电工、管道工等技术工人短缺，这些都是制约算力扩张的瓶颈。

**4. Stargate 战略全面展开**
OpenAI 采用"组合策略"：与 Microsoft、AWS、Google、Oracle 等云厂商合作，同时自建数据中心和定制芯片，确保算力来源多样化。

**5. MRC 网络协议**
针对 10 万+ GPU 规模的集群，OpenAI 开发了新的 MRC 协议，通过多路径流量分散技术，优雅处理大规模集群中的链路故障。

**6. 数据中心的社会价值**
数据中心建设为农村地区带来了新的房产税收入、基础设施升级和就业机会，对当地社区是净利好。

---

### Key Insights

**1. Compute Supply vs Demand Imbalance**
OpenAI's compute demands far outstrip supply. Sachin reveals the company plans to spend ~$5B on compute this year, with the entire industry expected to spend $70B. Any compute brought online is consumed immediately - no idle capacity.

**2. AI Designing Its Own Chips**
The Jalapeno chip went from design to tape-out in just 9 months, with AI playing a key role in design optimization. OpenAI believes the era of "recursion" - where AI designs the systems it needs - is near.

**3. Physical World Constraints**
Supply chains and factory construction can't keep up with AI demand growth. Transformer and gas turbine production capacity is severely limited, and there's a shortage of electricians, plumbers, and skilled workers.

**4. Stargate Strategy in Full Swing**
OpenAI uses a "portfolio approach": partnerships with Microsoft, AWS, Google, Oracle, plus custom-built data centers and chips to diversify compute sources.

**5. MRC Networking Protocol**
For clusters of 100,000+ GPUs, OpenAI developed the MRC protocol that gracefully handles failures through multipath traffic dispersion.

**6. Data Centers' Social Value**
Data center construction brings new property taxes, infrastructure upgrades, and jobs to rural areas - a net positive for local communities.
